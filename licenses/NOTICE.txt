Siege Lines
https://rosario.github.io/siege-lines/

Siege Lines is a tower defence built on Battle Drones by Simon Willison
(https://github.com/simonw/battledrones), used under the Apache License,
Version 2.0. A copy of the licence is in LICENSE.

Siege Lines' source holds all of Battle Drones (its own README is
README.md), with Siege Lines added on top.

Added for Siege Lines:
  siege.html, vite.siege.config.ts, TOWER-DEFENCE.md, PUBLISHING.md, NOTICE,
  .github/README.md, .github/workflows/publish.yml (publishes Siege Lines)
  src/siege/
  src/sim/modes/siege.ts, src/sim/siege-levels.ts, src/sim/siege-maps.ts,
  src/sim/siege-pilot.ts
  tests/siege.test.ts, tests/siege-pilot.ts
  tools/siege-balance.ts, tools/siege-input-check.mjs, tools/siege-maps-shots.mjs,
  tools/siege-shots.mjs, tools/siege-share-image.mjs

Battle Drones files changed for Siege Lines (the source files say so in
their first line):
  src/sim/types.ts, src/sim/sim.ts, src/sim/combat.ts, src/sim/movement.ts,
  src/sim/infantry.ts, src/sim/powers.ts, src/sim/salvage.ts
  src/versus/codec.ts
  src/game/render/entities.ts, src/game/three/screen.ts
  README.md (a section on Siege Lines)
  package.json, package-lock.json (Siege Lines' scripts and its two fonts)
  .gitignore (Siege Lines' build output)

Battle Drones files removed for Siege Lines:
  .github/workflows/pages.yml (publishes Battle Drones' own site)
